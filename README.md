Project Topic: Customer-Segmentation-Visualization-and-Churn-Analysis-

This is a churn analysis report on a telecommuication company. It showed the monthly distribution by active and churned customers. It also showed the churned rate, gender rate by tenure

Tools: Microsoft excel and PowerBi

Process: Microsoft excel was used for cleaning and PowerBi was ised for Visualization.

Key Findings: There were 5174 active customers and 1869 churned customers.

The churned rate continues to decrease as the tenure increases i.e. customers who stay longer are less likely to stay

Churn rate for both the genders are almost the same i.e., gender does not really affect churn rate

The heat map shows that most customers leave at their 1st to 6th tenure
